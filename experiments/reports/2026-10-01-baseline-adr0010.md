# Baseline re-run under the ADR 0010 Binance exclusion

## Why this re-run

The [September 27 baseline](2026-09-27-baseline.md) was produced at commit
`0dd437d`, before Binance became a signal-only venue. Its outputs show a
Binance leg on 11 of 12 orders (buy Binance / sell Lighter, and once buy Aster /
sell Binance). Those results violate
[ADR 0010](../../docs/adr/0010-binance-lead-aster-lighter-lag.md) and are
superseded by this report.

## Input and split

- Dataset: `6b556515-5156-4181-8a6f-7403fa9078fe`, Parquet schema 2, one
  complete Capture Run (2026-09-27 10:01:25.338 to 2026-09-28 09:03:43.613 UTC).
- Splits are unchanged: train Capture Sequence `[1, 2,403,822)`, validation
  `[2,403,822, 3,514,231)`. Both intervals were confirmed by the runner output.
- The test split has not been run.

## Code and configuration

Simulator at commit `f9e0d52`. Two behaviour changes since the old baseline:

1. **Binance exclusion.** `CrossVenueArbitrageStrategy` drops
   `signal_only_venues` (default `{"binance"}`) from the tradable set, and
   `ExchangeSimulator` rejects any intent on a signal-only venue.
2. **Order Book invalidation.** An `order_book` / `unavailable` availability
   event now removes that venue's last book from both the strategy view and the
   exchange simulator until the next snapshot.

The results below include both changes; their effects are not separated.

Parameters are otherwise the same as the old baseline, with no tuning: top one
level per side, 5 bps taker fee per venue, 50 ms order latency, zero added
market-data latency, zero slippage, required net edge of 1 bps plus a 1 bps
latency buffer. Initial cash is 1,000,000 USD per configured venue; equity
starts at 3,000,000 instead of 4,000,000 because Hyperliquid is no longer
configured. PnL figures are comparable; equity totals are not.

Commands:

```bash
python -m experiments.simulator.run \
  --dataset experiments/data/parquet/6b556515-5156-4181-8a6f-7403fa9078fe \
  --split train --max-depth 1 \
  --output experiments/data/results/day-train-top1-adr0010
python -m experiments.simulator.run \
  --dataset experiments/data/parquet/6b556515-5156-4181-8a6f-7403fa9078fe \
  --split validation --max-depth 1 \
  --output experiments/data/results/day-validation-top1-adr0010
```

Compliance checks on the new outputs: zero Binance orders, zero Binance fills
and zero rejected orders in both splits, so the strategy filter, not the
exchange rejection, enforced the exclusion. The single train opportunity
(net edge 2.04 bps, 0.0002 BTC) filled on both legs and left a flat net
position (+0.0002 BTC Aster, −0.0002 BTC Lighter), marked to market at the last
book rather than closed.

A second train run gave an identical `summary.json`.

## Interpretation

With Binance not tradable, almost every opportunity the old baseline found
disappears. At these settings an Aster ↔ Lighter trade needs more than 12 bps
of gross top-of-book spread (10 bps of fees plus the 2 bps threshold). That
happened once in roughly 23 hours of train data and never in validation.

This is **not evidence of an edge or of its absence**. One fill cannot support
any return estimate. The fees are placeholders, not the venues' real tiers, and
the latency is an assumption rather than a measurement. The strategy is also
still a pure Aster ↔ Lighter spread strategy: Binance books are visible, but
nothing yet uses Binance price moves as a lead signal, which is the hypothesis
the project actually targets.

## Next steps (not done here)

- Decide the partial two-leg execution and leftover-inventory policy before
  any test-split evaluation.
- Replace the placeholder fees with verified Aster and Lighter fee tiers.
- Replace the assumed latency with measured per-venue latency.
- Implement a Binance lead/lag signal; the current baseline doesn't test it.

## Re-run with the account tiers in use

The simulator defaults now match the accounts in use (checked 2026-10-01 against
the venue docs):

- Aster base tier: 4 bps taker.
- Lighter Standard: 0 bps taker, with a 300 ms venue delay on market orders
  (`taker_speed_bump_ns`). With the 50 ms order latency, Lighter market orders
  activate 350 ms after the decision; Aster's activate after 50 ms.

The commands are unchanged; the output goes to `day-{train,validation}-top1-tiers`.

| Split | Orders | Fills | Venue pairs (buy → sell) | Fees (USD) | Simulated net PnL (USD) | Ending net BTC |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Train | 4 | 4 | Aster → Lighter ×2 | 0.0135 | −0.0118 | 0 |
| Validation | 0 | 0 | — | 0 | 0 | 0 |

- **More signals, still no edge.** The required gross spread fell from about
  12 bps to about 6 bps (4 bps Aster fee, plus 0 bps Lighter, plus the 2 bps
  threshold), so train produced one more opportunity. Validation still has
  none.
- **Legging risk the strategy doesn't see.** The Lighter leg now fills 300 ms
  after the Aster leg, but the strategy still prices both legs from the same
  quote. This baseline is a pure spread strategy and remains a weak candidate.
